About

I focus on the architecture and shared foundations other engineers rely on: design systems, authentication and component libraries. I make technical decisions with the whole company in mind, from what other teams depend on to what the business needs.

I own delivery end to end, from technical concept and estimation through go-live, observability and incident response. I keep products fast and monitored, measure how they're used with product analytics, and keep codebases consistent through code review. I mentor developers and work in Agile, cross-functional teams with OKR-based planning.

I work in TypeScript, React and Node.js on AWS, with a Java background. I've fully adopted AI-assisted development (Claude, GitHub Copilot) and review its output like any other code.

I'm currently a Senior Web Engineer at Carly in Munich, working across its cloud applications, services and AI-assisted chat agent. Before that I built products at DemoUp Cliplister, Holidu, Reltio and others, in Germany and India.
